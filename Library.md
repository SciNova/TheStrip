# Library

## Apps

### WhiteLight

A plain white lamp with smooth fade

- Toggle (FIRE, FIRE2, A, B, C, D)
- Level (UP/DOWN, LEFT/RIGHT)

### ColorLight

A dual white/color mode lamp with smooth fades.

- Toggle (FIRE, A, B, C, D)
- Level (UP/DOWN)
- Mode (FIRE2)
- Temperature (RIGHT/LEFT)

### Moodlight

An ambient plasma lamp with themes.

- Theme (FIRE, FIRE2, A, B, C, D)
- Speed (UP/DOWN, RIGHT/LEFT)

Themes: sky, mature, fireplace

### DemoScene

A tribute to the 90s demoscene.

- Next (UP, RIGHT, FIRE, FIRE2, A, B, C, D)
- Prev: (LEFT, DOWN).

Effects: Fractal Mountain, Starfield Warp, Fireworks, Interference, Aurora Borealis, Digital Rain, Copper Bars, Scanner, Reactive Burst, Bouncing Balls.

### Dices

A pair of rolling dice.

### StopWatch

A stopwatch, with dice faces.


## Games

### Darts

A timing game modeled on real darts. A needle sweeps back and forth across a dartboard of concentric rings; starting the sweep and then stopping it throws a dart wherever the needle currently is, scoring according to which ring it lands in. It gets progressively harder as play goes on.

### Dino

The Chrome dinosaur runner. An endless runner where oncoming obstacles of varying height must be jumped over.

### FlappyBird

A bird falls under gravity and flaps upward, needing to pass through a gap in terrain closing in from both sides, which narrows and grows more erratic over time.

### Pacman

The classic Pac-Man. The player eats pellets while hunted by four ghosts, each with its own chase style; a magic pellet turns the hunt around, letting the ghosts be eaten instead.

Ghosts: Blinky, Pinky, Inky, Clyde.

### Stack

The player defends against an approaching line of colored pixels (snake) that drifts steadily toward the player's fixed position at one end of the strip. The snake is composed of a sequence of red, green, and blue dots separated by gaps.

When a bullet of the correct color is fired, the leading dot is removed and the snake shortens. If the bullet's color doesn't match the leading dot, it instead becomes a new dot added to the front of the snake, lengthening it and bringing it closer to the player. The snake's drift speed and number of dots increase with each level, while the gaps between dots decrease.

### Slots

A three-reel fruit machine. Spinning settles each reel on a random symbol, and three matching symbols across the centre is a win.

Symbols: Apple, Banana, Grape, Orange, Cherry, Lemon, Watermelon, Blackberry, Strawberry.

### Race

Two players race side by side, going back and forth across the full length, each full pass counting as a lap. Whoever completes the required number of laps first wins.

### SimonSays

The classic memory game. A sequence of coloured signals grows by one each level, and must be repeated back correctly; a mistake, or taking too long, replays the level, and completing the full sequence finishes it.


## Drafts
