# 28 Jan 2026 – DeepSeek 3 – RACE: physics engine, speed trails, and two-player racing

RACE was built from scratch. A `Player` class tracks position, speed, direction, and laps. Friction is velocity-dependent (exponential decay); speed is capped at 180 pixels/second, the theoretical maximum at 15 button presses per second. Boundaries reflect rather than wrap — proper bounce physics.

The trail system is speed-dependent: length scales from 1 to 20 pixels, with a brightness gradient from a full-intensity head (255) to a dim tail (127). Trail drawing is handled in a single bounce-aware loop with no simulation — purely geometric. At high friction the trail produces a "radioactive worm" effect that makes speed immediately legible.

Player A (green) is controlled by the left button, Player B (blue) by the right. Both share the 120-pixel strip with overlapping trails; laps are counted on the left boundary bounce. Core gameplay is complete and playable.
