# 30 Jan 2026 – DeepSeek 3 – DICES: rotating square projection and the 4-sides-6-faces illusion

### Projection engine

We built a real-time 3D projection engine for a rotating square on the 1D strip. The projection width varies with rotation angle — 15 px when flat, ~21 px at 45° diagonal — and brightness scales with the pixel's simulated height in 3D space. The key mathematics:

- `length1 = |cosθ| × faceSize`, `length2 = |sinθ| × faceSize`
- `y = xFace × sinθ` for each pixel
- Intensity: `(y + maxY) / (2×maxY)` where `maxY = faceSize × √2 / 2`

### The 6-faces illusion

The harder problem was representing six dice faces on a four-sided square. The solution: rotate 540° (1.5 full turns) to cycle through all six faces, with the face order `[1, 3, 5, 2, 6, 4]` for a logical progression. The illusion holds. Dice patterns are 15-pixel arrays with black dots on a green background; both body and dots scale intensity with 3D position. Manual rotation is one button press per 1/60 turn. The `Dice` class has four states — Idle, Rolling, Stopping, Falling — with acceleration, deceleration, and snapping to the nearest face.
