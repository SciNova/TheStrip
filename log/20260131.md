# 31 Jan 2026 – DeepSeek 3 – Strip3D: cylindrical LEDs, spherical camera, and professional lighting

### LED meshes and camera

The Three.js visualiser was rebuilt properly. Sphere geometries were replaced with accurate cylindrical LED meshes (diameter 0.8, height 0.1), 120 of them in formation along the Z-axis. The camera system uses spherical coordinates (phi, theta, radius) orbiting a fixed look-at point at the strip centre (z=−60), opening at 65° from above — optimised for POV game recording.

### Controls

The full control scheme:

- Arrows: orbit camera
- Shift + Arrows: pan camera and look-at point (camera-relative)
- Ctrl + Up/Down: zoom
- Ctrl + Left/Right: rotate strip around Z-axis
- F10: toggle between DOM and Three.js renderers
- 1/2/3/4: simulate controller buttons

### Lighting

Lighting uses multiple sources — directional, ambient, fill — plus a camera-following point light for consistent illumination as the view moves. LEDs have emissive material properties for a glow effect; black pixels render as dark grey for visibility.
