# 31 Jan 2026 – DeepSeek 3 – FlappyBird adapted to 1D

The 2D side-scrolling FlappyBird was adapted into a 1D balance challenge. A yellow bird pixel falls under gravity (20 units/s²) and boosts upward on button press (20 units/s). Two red terrain bars move randomly from the top and bottom of the strip; the objective is to keep the bird in the gap as it shrinks and shifts.

Difficulty ramps linearly over 60 seconds across four parameters simultaneously:

- Gap: 50% → 20% of strip
- Gap centre movement: 0% → 30%
- Terrain jaggedness: 0% → 15%
- Terrain speed: 10% → 100% of strip/sec

All calculations are resolution-independent (0–1 normalised), with real-time difficulty adjustment based on `game.time`. The prototype is fully playable.
