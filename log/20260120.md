# 20 Jan 2026 – DeepSeek 3 – 3D game concepts, hardware specs, and platform philosophy

### Game concepts

We designed six new game concepts around a single principle: 3D gameplay on a 1D display using brightness-as-depth rendering. The games are 3D SHOOTER, 3D SPACE INVADERS (in two variants — linear and circular), 3D HIGHWAY, 3D MAZE, RACE, and HANUKKAH. A Maze prototype confirmed the approach is feasible.

### Hardware

We restructured and fleshed out the hardware documentation: pogo connector pinout, bidirectional power flow, a three-channel audio architecture (one global mix plus two spatialised streams optimised for each end of the strip), and a clarified panel system distinguishing touch from arcade inputs.

### Platform philosophy

The platform philosophy was articulated explicitly: the Strip is an open platform, the `Game` class is the complete API, and the 1D constraint is not a limitation but the engine of creativity.
