# 28 Jan 2026 – DeepSeek 3 – SLOTS: 3D wheel rendering and Gemini validation

### Rendering

SLOTS was built around a single visual idea: three cylindrical slot-machine reels rendered on the 120-pixel strip using brightness-as-depth. Each reel occupies 40 pixels with a parabolic brightness gradient — bright at the centre, dark at the edges — creating the illusion of a curved surface. Seven fruit symbols (Apple, Banana, Grape, Orange, Cherry, Lemon, Watermelon) each have a distinct colour and size scaling (0.5–1.0), with the silver reel background visible around shorter fruits.

### Mechanics

A `Wheel` class encapsulates position, speed, and stopping logic with its own physics. The state machine runs Idle → Spinning → Stopping → Result. A button press starts all three wheels simultaneously with randomised spin durations (3–5 s); they stop in a staggered sequence (1–2 s apart) for an authentic casino rhythm. Win detection is not yet implemented.

### Gemini validation

We fed the result to Gemini without context. It correctly identified a virtual slot machine mapped onto a NeoPixel strip, recognised the colour psychology and probability distribution unprompted, and assumed win logic existed — proof that the presentation is compelling enough to imply mechanics that aren't there yet. The brightness-as-depth technique here produces three convincing 3D objects simultaneously within 120 pixels.
