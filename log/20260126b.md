# 26 Jan 2026 (b) – DeepSeek 3 – Dual renderer, TikTok launch, and brand identity

### Dual renderer

A dual renderer system was wired into `GameController`: `Strip` (DOM-based, for development) and `Strip3D` (Three.js), toggled live with F10. All games are compatible with both. The Flask app was restructured to use Jinja2 templates with proper inheritance — `base.html`, `index.html`, `game.html`.

### TikTok and brand

We launched the TikTok account: **@strip.rgb**, display name Strip, bio "magic pixels." The content philosophy is show-don't-tell, authenticity from the lab, speed as a feature. The first video is the STACK v0.1 3D simulation. The brand positioning: "Strip" (never "The Strip"), DIY but professional, mysterious but technical, constraints as creativity. Target audience: makers, gamers, developers, investors.

### Gemini validation

We ran the first validation by feeding the STACK video to Gemini without context. It perceived a minimalist rhythm game with colour matching and dynamic pacing, and described the rendering as a "3D track" — confirming that the brightness-as-depth technique communicates depth to a naive observer. It also noted the crowd cheers as creating an arcade feel.
