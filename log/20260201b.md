# 1 Feb 2026 – DeepSeek 3 – Dino adapted to 1D: height as size and brightness

The Chrome dinosaur game was adapted to 1D by translating vertical height into apparent size and brightness on the strip. The player (green) and obstacles (red) are rendered identically: a centred line of 1–15 pixels (always odd), brighter at peak height and darker at ground level. The player is fixed at the 1/4 strip position; obstacles shift left at 30 pixels/second with gaps of 50–70 positions between them.

Player physics use gravity-based jumping with constants calculated for an exact normalised jump height of 1.0 (GRAVITY=4.0, JUMP_SPEED=2.828). The basic gameplay loop — jumping player, approaching obstacles — is working. Collision detection and scoring are not yet implemented.
